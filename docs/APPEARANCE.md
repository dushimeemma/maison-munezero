# Appearance

Use the appearance icon in the top bar to choose **Device default**, **Light**,
or **Dark**. It is available to guests, customers and staff, including product,
shopping bag, sign-in and order detail screens.

Device default is the initial setting and follows the operating system or
browser's colour preference, including changes while the app is open. Explicit
Light and Dark settings override that preference. The selection is stored on
this device and restored before the app opens; signing out does not remove it.
It does not change another user's preference or the shop's business settings.
If storage is unavailable, the app starts in Device default. A failed change
shows a message and restores the previous setting.

Both themes use the same Maison typography and green/ivory palette. Panels,
forms, navigation, secondary text and status chips use their theme colours.
Garment illustrations and the green atelier banners retain their intended colours.

Email appearance is independent of the app's preference: email clients control
how they display HTML and dark mode. See EMAIL.md for the branded email layout.
